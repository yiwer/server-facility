The application Maven version and template revision `secured-api/2026.10.0` are independent. No jar was relabeled and neither line was published by this work. Record actual jar checksums beside mutable SNAPSHOT coordinates.

Known consumers are the independent consumer projects, historical binary fixtures, partner-aggregation example, copied secured-api application and explicitly recorded historical customization sample. Public releases/tags and Maven Central markers were absent in the 2026-10-04 read-only inventory; private registries and source-copy consumers remain unknown. No justified old-line sunset date or maintenance-release promise follows. Inventory real downstream callers before removing deprecated entry points.
